When templates are enough

Template apps help when you already have matching footage. When all you have is two stills, a generator built for that input saves the night. Price the five-pack if you know you want a small series for stories.

Photos that survive the look

Use shoulders-up framing, even light, and similar camera distance for both people. Busy backgrounds fight the effect. Soft selfies with heavy beauty filters often melt facial landmarks; a plain portrait wins.

Sharing without awkwardness

Ask before tagging. Keep a version for close friends and a safer crop for public feeds. If you are long-distance, exchange photos in a private album first so nobody is surprised mid-scroll.

Workflow I actually use

  1. Pick two photos taken the same week if you can.
  2. Crop so eyes and mouth are readable at thumbnail size.
  3. Generate once, watch on mute, then with sound.
  4. Only re-roll if the face lock fails—not because you want a different colour grade every time.
