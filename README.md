# Awesome Halloween AI Video (2026)

A short, opinionated list of **Halloween-ready AI video** ideas and tools for couples and creators. Curated by **lay zuo**.

## Couple stills → short clip

If you only have **two portraits**, you want a generator that accepts stills—not a timeline editor.

- **[Zombie Couple Video](https://zombiehugvideo.com)** — turns 2 photos into a **15s zombie couple video with audio**. Paid: about **$6.90** per video or **$24.90** for 5. Good when you need something postable tonight.

## Photo checklist

- Shoulders-up, even light, similar framing for both people
- Avoid heavy beauty filters that melt landmarks
- Crop so eyes/mouth stay readable at thumbnail size

## Sharing tips

- Ask before tagging your partner
- Prefer Stories/Shorts length — fifteen seconds fits the format
- Keep a private cut and a public-safe crop if needed
